# College Roadmap App – Case Study

## Overview
The College Roadmap App is designed to help high school students plan, track, and achieve their academic and extracurricular milestones on the path to college readiness. It provides a personalized dashboard, milestone tracking, reminders, and resources to support students from Grade 9 through Grade 12.

## Problem Statement
Many students and their families struggle to keep track of the complex requirements and deadlines involved in preparing for college. Missing key milestones can impact college admissions and scholarship opportunities.

## Solution
The app offers a centralized platform where students can:
- View grade-specific milestones and tasks
- Track their progress across academic years
- Receive important reminders and notifications
- Access curated resources for college preparation
- Visualize their journey with a timeline and progress indicators

## Key Features
- **Personalized Dashboard:** Displays upcoming tasks, progress, and quick actions.
- **Milestone Catalog:** Lists grade-specific academic, extracurricular, and application milestones.
- **Progress Tracking:** Allows students to mark milestones as complete and monitor their advancement.
- **Reminders:** Sends notifications for important deadlines and events.
- **Resource Library:** Provides articles, guides, and links relevant to college planning.
- **Chatbot:** Offers instant answers to common questions and guidance.
- **Profile Management:** Lets users update their information and preferences.

## Value Proposition
- Reduces stress by organizing the college preparation process
- Increases student engagement and accountability
- Improves the likelihood of meeting all college application requirements
- Empowers students with knowledge and timely support

## Target Users
- High school students (Grades 9–12)
- Parents and guardians
- School counselors and educators

## Technology Stack
- **Frontend:** React (TypeScript)
- **Backend:** Node.js with Express
- **Database:** (Details in server/db.ts)

## Conclusion
The College Roadmap App streamlines the journey to college, making it easier for students to stay on track and achieve their goals. By providing structure, reminders, and resources, it supports student success every step of the way.
